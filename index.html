Live Scan OCR — Prototype

Point the camera at a screen and slow-scroll or pause on rows. It grabs a frame only when motion settles, runs OCR, matches text against a sample dairy product list, and tallies quantities. This is a standalone test — nothing here touches the real tracker yet.

How the matching works in this demo: it looks for lines shaped like PRODUCT NAME ... NUMBER and matches the product text against a small sample list (Chobani, Fairlife, Stew Leonard's Milk, etc.) using fuzzy matching, then reads the trailing number as the quantity. In the real integration, the sample list would be swapped for your live dairy case product table, and the parsing pattern would be tuned to whatever the actual sales screen's column layout looks like.
